Admission

Admission Process

  1. check program eligibility.
  2. select your prefered academic program.
  3. submit the application form.
  4. complete the online application process.
  5. upload the required documents.
  6. check the merit list or admission status.
  7. Attend counseling if required.
  8. Complete fee payment and admission formalities.
  9. Receive your admission number.

Important Terms

Eligibilty
The academic qualifications and other conditions that a student must satisfy to apply for a particular program.
Merit List
A list of applicants prepared according to their academic performance or admission criteria.
Counseling
A process where students receive guidance and support regarding their academic and career decisions.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students based on academic performance or other applicable criteria
Admission Number
A unique identification number assigned to each student upon successful admission to the university.
