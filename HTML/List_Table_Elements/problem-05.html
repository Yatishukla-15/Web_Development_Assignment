Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to the official portal of our Annual College Fest 2026. Join us for three exciting days filled with competitions, cultural performances, technology events, fun activities and much more.

Fest Introduction

Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day celebration organized by the student committee. The fest provides students with an opportunity to participate in cultural, technical, sports and entertainment activities.

Students from different colleges are welcome to participate and showcase their creativity, talent and teamwork.

Registration Process

Follow these steps to successfully register for the fest.

  1. Visit the official registration page.
  2. Click on the "Register Now" button.
  3. Enter your name, college, email and contact number.
  4. Select the competition in which you want to participate.
  5. Upload the required documents if applicable.
  6. Pay the registration fee.
  7. Submit the registration form.
  8. Save the registration confirmation for future reference.

Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Registration must be completed before the deadline.
  • Participants must report at the venue at least 30 minutes before the event.
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice will result in disqualification.
  • Participants must follow the instructions given by coordinators.
  • Damage to college property is strictly prohibited.
  • Decisions of the judging panel will be final.
  • Participants must maintain discipline and cleanliness.

Important Fest Terms

Open Event
An event where students from different colleges can participate.
Team Event
An event where two or more participants compete together as a team.
Individual Event
An event where a participant competes alone.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator
The person responsible for managing and conducting an event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where selected participants compete for the final positions.
