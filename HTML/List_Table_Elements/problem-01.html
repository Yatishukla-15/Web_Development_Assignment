University Admission Handbook

Admission overview

Welcome to GLA University Admission Handbook. This handbook provides important information about the admission procedure, eligibility requirements, required documents, important admission terms, fee structure, and contact details.

Admission office message: We welcome all students who wish to begin their higher education journey with GLA University. please carefully read all admisssion requirements and complete the application process before the deadline.

Eligibility

Students applying for admission must fulfill the eligibility requirements of their selected program.

  • 10+2 qualification from a recognised board for undergraduate program.
  • Required subjects according to the selected course.
  • Minimum percentage requirements as specified for the program.
  • For postgraduate programs, a relevant bachelor's degree may be required.
    • Application Deadline:30 June 2027

Application process

Follow these admission steps in sequence:

  1. visit the official university admission website.
  2. Register using a valid email address and mobile number.
  3. Fill in the online application form.
  4. upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Sumbit the completed application form.
  7. Check the merit list and counselling information.
  8. complete admission and registration after selection.

Required Documents

  • class 10 marksheet and certificate
  • class 12 marksheet and certificates
  • Transfer certificate
  • Migration certificate, if applicable
  • Government-issued identity proof
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Entrance examinations scorecard, if applicable
  • Category certificate, if applicable

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum academic and other requirements that a student must satisfy to apply for a particular program.
Merit list
A list of applicants prepared according to their academic performance or other admission options.
Counselling
A process in which eligible students receive information and guidance about available programs and admission options.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students based on academic performance or other specified criteria.
Registration
The process of officialy enrolling a selected student in the university or program.
