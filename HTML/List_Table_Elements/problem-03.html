Practical Class Sequence

Students should follow these steps before attending a practical class:

  1. Check the practical timetable and laboratory number.
  2. Read the experiment instructions before entering the lab.
  3. Bring the required notebook and study material.
  4. Report to the laboratory on time.
  5. Follow the faculty's safety and practical instructions.
  6. Complete the experiment and record the observations.
  7. Submit the practical work or laboratory record as instructed.

Academic Rules

  • Students must maintain the required attendance percentage.
  • Students must carry their college ID card.
  • Students must arrive on time for lectures and practicals.
  • Maintain discipline inside classrooms and laboratories.
  • Mobile phones should be used only when permitted by faculty.
  • Students must keep laboratories and classrooms clean.
  • Assignments and practical records must be submitted before the deadline.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session where the faculty explains theoretical concepts to students.
Lab
A practical learning session conducted in a laboratory.
Tutorial
A smaller academic session focused on practice, problem-solving, and discussion.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, attendance, presentations, or other academic activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic session where students apply theoretical concepts through experiments or activities.
